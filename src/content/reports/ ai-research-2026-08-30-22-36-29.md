## 💡 Project & Startup Ideas

### 01 — Global South ASR Multi-Lingual Speech App

**Problem:** Lack of robust speech recognition and benchmarking datasets for Global South languages in consumer applications.

**Why now:** The Open ASR Leaderboard recently added its first Global South language, providing a foundation for inclusive evaluation and speech AI development.

**Difficulty:** Medium

### 02 — Compressed 4-Bit Edge Model Deployment Pipeline

**Problem:** Running uncompressed large language models on edge devices is hardware-intensive and costly.

**Why now:** New techniques like Quantization-Aware Healing allow 4-bit compressed models to outperform their full-precision originals, boosting edge efficiency.

**Difficulty:** Hard

### 03 — Advanced Multi-Vector Retrieval System

**Problem:** Traditional single-vector embedding models struggle to capture nuanced, multi-faceted context in complex retrieval tasks.

**Why now:** New tutorials and tools are available for training and finetuning multi-vector embedding models using sentence transformers.

**Difficulty:** Medium
