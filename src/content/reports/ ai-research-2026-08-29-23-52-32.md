## 💡 Project & Startup Ideas

### 01 — Global South Speech Recognition Evaluator

**Problem:** Limited automated speech recognition (ASR) benchmarking and performance data for languages from the Global South.

**Why now:** The Open ASR Leaderboard has just added its first Global South language, highlighting an urgent need for broader linguistic inclusion and tooling.

**Difficulty:** Medium

### 02 — Multi-Vector Document Retrieval Assistant

**Problem:** Standard dense embedding models often fail to capture nuanced, multi-faceted relationships in complex enterprise documents.

**Why now:** New techniques and tools for training and finetuning multi-vector embedding models with sentence transformers are now available.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Problem:** Enterprises need transparent, specialized open models to reason over proprietary data and internal workflows.
