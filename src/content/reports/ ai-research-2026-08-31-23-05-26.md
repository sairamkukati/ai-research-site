## 💡 Project & Startup Ideas

### 01 — Ultra-Low-Bit Mobile Assistant

**Problem:** Running powerful LLMs locally on edge devices typically requires high memory and degrades model accuracy.

**Why now:** Recent advancements in Quantization-Aware Healing allow compressed 4-bit models to outperform their uncompressed original baselines.

**Difficulty:** Hard

### 02 — Global South Speech-to-Text Localizer

**Problem:** Automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems often lack robust evaluation and performance for diverse Global South languages.

**Why now:** The Open ASR Leaderboard has officially added its first Global South language, providing standard benchmarking tools.

**Difficulty:** Medium

### 03 — Multimodal Itinerary Builder

**Problem:** Planning and booking trips manually across multiple fragmented web services is tedious and time-consuming.

**Why now:** Google has recently introduced multimodal AI agent capabilities into search for travel planning and booking.

**Difficulty:** Easy
