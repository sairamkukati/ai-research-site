## 💡 Project & Startup Ideas

### 01 — Global South Voice-to-Text Localizer

**Problem:** Limited speech recognition evaluation and support for Global South languages in existing open-source ASR tools.

**Why now:** The Open ASR Leaderboard has just added its first Global South language, opening up evaluation infrastructure for underrepresented tongues.

**Difficulty:** Medium

### 02 — Advanced Multi-Vector Document Retrieval Engine

**Problem:** Standard embedding models often fail to capture fine-grained semantic nuances across complex documents.

**Why now:** New techniques and workflows for training and finetuning multi-vector embedding models with sentence transformers are now available.

**Difficulty:** Hard

### 03 — AI-Powered Interactive Study and Planning Assistant

**Problem:** Students and travelers lack unified interfaces that combine structured search insights with intelligent planning tools.

**Why now:** Recent developments in search platforms demonstrate active consumer integration of AI features for structured learning and travel planning.

**Difficulty:** Easy
